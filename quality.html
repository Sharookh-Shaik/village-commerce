QUALITY & PROCESS

Clear specifications. Confirmed for the order.

For agricultural products, grade and lot matter. Our buyer process is designed to confirm the details that matter before quotation and fulfilment.

01

Requirement

Buyer shares variety, quantity, destination, form and preferred packaging.

02

Product matching

We identify the relevant grade and sourcing requirement.

03

Quality details

Relevant parameters such as moisture, colour, pungency, size and form are confirmed for the proposed lot where applicable.

04

Commercial terms

Price, quantity, packing, delivery terms and documentation are discussed for the specific order.

05

Fulfilment

Once terms are agreed, order preparation and applicable documentation proceed accordingly.
